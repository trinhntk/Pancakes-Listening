Bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh (136 từ · in đậm = từ nối)

The pictures illustrate the process of making pancakes.

Overall, there are nine stages, beginning with weighing the ingredients and ending with serving the pancakes. The process can be divided into two main phases: preparing the batter and cooking the pancakes.

In the first stage, all the ingredients are weighed carefully. Flour, salt and sugar are then put into a large bowl, while the egg, milk and oil are combined in a separate bowl. Next, the liquid ingredients are slowly added to the flour mixture and stirred constantly until a smooth, thick batter is formed.

In the cooking phase, a frying pan is heated and lightly greased with butter. Once the butter has melted, a large spoonful of batter is poured into the pan and cooked until the edges turn brown. At this point, the pancake is turned over and cooked for about one more minute. It is then placed on a plate and covered to keep it warm while the remaining pancakes are made. Finally, the pancakes are served with syrup, sugar and lemon, or fruit.
